Build three: the UD-slice coordinate

The cube has 12 edges. Four of them — the slice edges — belong in the equator between the U and D faces. This coordinate tracks only which 4 of the 12 edge slots they currently sit in.

Click a teal edge to pick it up, then click any other slot to move it there. Goal: get all four slice edges into the shaded equator.

Why it ignores the order. Phase 1 only needs the four slice edges back in the equator — not in any particular arrangement. So we count which 4 slots, not their order: a combination (495), not a permutation (11 880). One special value means "all four are home" — that's the phase-1 target.

"All I ask in phase 1 is: are the four slice edges home in the middle layer? That single question — sharpened to which 4 of 12 slots they occupy — is the UD-slice coordinate."
